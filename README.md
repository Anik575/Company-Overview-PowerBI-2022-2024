# Company-Overview-PowerBI-2022-2024
Power BI project analyzing company performance from 2022 to 2024 using Excel data. The project demonstrates business intelligence techniques through data modeling, visualization, and dashboarding. Insights include revenue by country, revenue vs. expenses, product-wise revenue, quarterly revenue trends, and profit contribution by managers.

This repository contains a Power BI project analyzing a company's performance between **2022 and 2024** based on Excel data.  

The project highlights key financial and business insights such as:  
- Revenue trends by product and country  
- Comparison of revenue and expenses  
- Quarterly revenue performance  
- Profit contribution by managers  

## Methodology & Implementation  

### Data Preparation  
- Imported and transformed Excel data.  
- Cleaned and standardized columns for analysis.  

### Data Modeling  
- Built relationships between tables for structured analysis.  
- Applied measures to calculate revenue, expenses, and profits.  

### Visualization & Dashboarding  
Created multiple report pages using various visuals:  
- **Bar & Column Charts** → Revenue by products and countries  
- **Line Charts** → Quarterly revenue trends across years  
- **Pie/Donut Charts** → Expense vs. Revenue overview  
- **TreeMap** → Profit contribution by managers  
- **Cards & KPIs** → Snapshot of total revenue, total expenses, and overall profit  


## Key Findings  

 **Revenue by Products & Country**  
- Products contributed differently to overall revenue depending on the region.  

 **Revenue vs. Expenses Overview**  
- Visualizations showed cost-effectiveness and profitability over three years.  

 **Quarterly Revenue Trends**  
- Seasonal and quarterly performance patterns identified.  

 **Profit Contribution by Managers**  
- Clear picture of managerial impact on profitability.
